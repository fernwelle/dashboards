Expert Nutritionist Notes
Single biggest gap: protein. Without dairy or protein powder, this plan falls 20–45g short daily. A pea+rice blend (25g/scoop) is the cleanest fix — neutral taste, complete amino profile, no dairy. Non-negotiable if muscle building is a real goal at 41.
Calories too low on rest days (~1,400 kcal). Aggressive deficits on non-training days risk muscle loss and metabolic adaptation. Keep rest day calories at ~1,600 kcal minimum by adding a slightly larger dinner.
Home workout day mini-fuel is intentional. Dates + walnuts + pumpkin seeds at 5:45AM provides just enough glycogen to protect muscle during resistance training without disrupting the fasted state from overnight. Skip it only on walk days.
Meal 2 is the nutritional cornerstone. The rajma+chana+protein anchor combination is excellent — complete protein, high fiber, good micronutrients, batch-prep friendly. Protect this meal across all day types.
Pumpkin seeds across 3–4 meals is intentional. Zinc and magnesium are commonly deficient in high-training males over 40. This plan delivers a meaningful daily dose through food rather than supplements.
Fruit dinner (max 1x/week) is fine. Useful psychological release that keeps adherence high. Don't stack it on a day where chia jar was also skipped — protein will fall below 100g.
This plan aligns well with LM Kapoor's whole food framework while correcting its protein blindspot. Anti-inflammatory, high-fiber, low-processed-food foundation with enough animal protein to support real body recomposition at 41.
