Expert Nutritionist Notes
Single biggest gap: protein. Without dairy or protein powder, this plan consistently falls 20–45g short. A pea+rice blend (25g/scoop) is the cleanest fix — neutral taste, complete amino profile, no dairy. Non-negotiable if muscle building is a real goal at 41.
Calories may be too low on rest days (~1,400 kcal). Aggressive deficits on non-training days risk muscle loss and metabolic adaptation. Consider keeping rest day calories at ~1,600 kcal minimum by adding a slightly larger dinner.
Meal 2 is the nutritional cornerstone. The rajma+chana+protein anchor combination is excellent — complete protein, high fiber, good micronutrients, batch-prep friendly. Protect this meal; don't skip or reduce it.
Pumpkin seeds across 3 meals is intentional. Zinc and magnesium are commonly deficient in high-training males over 40. This plan delivers a meaningful daily dose through food rather than supplements.
Fruit dinner (max 1x/week) is fine. It's a useful psychological release valve that keeps adherence high. Just don't stack it with the chia jar being skipped — you'll end up under 100g protein that day.
Sprouts at dinner is a smart addition. Easy to digest, supports gut health, small protein contribution, and enzymes aid overnight recovery. Good habit.
This plan aligns well with LM Kapoor's whole food framework while correcting its protein blindspot. You're getting the anti-inflammatory, high-fiber, low-processed-food foundation with enough animal protein layered on top to support real body recomposition.
